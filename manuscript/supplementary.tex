\section{Evidence-layer and scope controls}
Historical measurements (H0), recovered original-code reproduction (R1), and the new study (N2) were stored separately. The N2 protocol hash, data-role receipts, code and environment hashes, run rosters, failed-run records, and test-access ledger are included in the reproducibility archive. W04 independent human review and W13 corrected-label interaction were excluded by the project decision owner before N2 outcome access. This exclusion limits all bounding-box results to the operational \texttt{inherited\_bbox\_region} target and prohibits physical-label-validity claims.

\section{Dataset manifest and grouping}
The derived manifest contains 5,632 file-hash-linked rows from 176 specimens. Each specimen contributes 32 retained images. The frozen split contains 112 training, 32 validation, and 32 held-out specimens, and views belonging to a specimen remain together. The supplement reports duplicate screening, condition ranges, box geometry checks, and all known data-quality limitations without redistributing licensed pixels.
