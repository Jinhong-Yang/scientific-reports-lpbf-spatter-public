\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider our Article, ``Optical residual regularization for LPBF spatter synthesis and detection,'' by Hyojin Park, Nam-Hyun Yoo, and Jinhong Yang.

The work addresses a technical question in manufacturing-image machine learning: whether lowering a dimensionless optical residual improves synthetic-image fidelity or the utility of those images for detector training once donor support, inherited supervision, real-image exposure, model family, and compute are controlled. The study separates historical and newly generated evidence, freezes its new-study protocol before held-out outcome access, retains negative and failed results, and reports specimen-grouped uncertainty. This emphasis on scientific validity and technically sound analysis is aligned with the publication criteria of \textit{Scientific Reports}.
