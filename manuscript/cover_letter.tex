\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider our Article, ``Optical residual regularization for LPBF spatter synthesis and detection,'' by Hyojin Park, Nam-Hyun Yoo, and Jinhong Yang.

The work addresses a technical question in manufacturing-image machine learning: whether optical-residual synthesis provides detector utility beyond donor-matched and real-exposure controls. Under the internally frozen protocol, the regularized arm exceeded the donor blend but was inferior to additional real-image exposure; comparisons with the data-only field and conventional augmentation remained uncertain. We present this as a controlled internal evaluation of conditional utility and limitations, not a superiority or calibrated-physics claim. The study retains failed results and reports specimen-grouped primary uncertainty. The test role had been used historically, inherited box semantics remain unverified, and no external cohort or independent rater evaluation is claimed.
