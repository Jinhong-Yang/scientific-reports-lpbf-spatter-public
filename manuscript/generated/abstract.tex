Synthetic imagery may expand training data for laser powder bed fusion monitoring, but an image-domain residual is not evidence of calibrated process physics. We tested a conditional coordinate-field generator with a dimensionless optical residual against data-only, donor-matched non-neural, smoothing, diffusion, conventional-augmentation, and additional-real-exposure controls. The frozen study used 5,632 images from 176 specimens, specimen-grouped train/validation/test roles, ten paired full-pipeline replicates, and pooled COCO AP@[.50:.95] on one locked held-out campaign. The four primary paired AP contrasts were NP-NB +0.006 (CI +0.001 to +0.012); NP-NF -0.001 (CI -0.007 to +0.004); NP-N1 -0.008 (CI -0.017 to +0.001); NP-NR -0.010 (CI -0.019 to -0.001). At least one multiplicity-controlled interval excluded zero; the direction and scope are reported without equivalence or external-validity claims. Low-data, resolution, architecture, and grouped out-of-fold analyses were secondary. All conclusions concern the inherited operational bounding-box target; particle semantics and external validity were not independently established.
