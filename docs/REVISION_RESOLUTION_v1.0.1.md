# Author-side revision resolution - v1.0.1

Scope: non-independent internal review. No independent rater, evaluator or
peer-review process was commissioned. This document supersedes the earlier
automatic readiness inference, not the frozen experiment records.

## Scientific assessment

The defensible contribution is a controlled internal comparison of synthesis
utility against donor-matched and additional-real-exposure controls. NP-NB is
positive under the four-comparison adjustment; NP-NR is negative. NP-NF and
NP-N1 remain uncertain. Effects are small on the AP scale and should not be
promoted to operational deployment benefit. The article is not evidence of
calibrated process physics, independently verified particle labels, external
generalization, or general superiority of neural synthesis.

Within this deliberately bounded contribution, the identified reporting and
production deficiencies have been addressed. The internal minor-revision-level
judgment is conditional on the hash-bound numeric, isolated-rebuild and visual
receipts passing. It is not a prediction of an editor's or peer reviewer's
decision. A journal can reasonably request stronger target validation, source
code access, or an independent cohort; those would change the evidence scope
and are not represented as already solved.

## Issues examined and resolutions

1. Public reconstruction failed because test and resolution aggregate matrices
   were absent. Both tables contain run identifiers, hashes and scalar metrics,
   not source images, boxes, or raw predictions. They are now explicit inputs
   in the allowlist and missing-input preflight regression tests. A separate
   outside-checkout test exercises preflight, rebuilding, and public tests,
   comparing regenerated text, CSVs and figure pixels. Full retraining and
   raw-prediction bootstrap reproduction remain outside the no-source-data route.
2. Methods were compared to actual frozen configuration and source. The ratio
   grid was not performed: 0.25 was the preapproved fallback. NP uses F11 while
   NF uses F00; this changes all regularizers, not only the optical term. NS
   starts from F01 plus sigma-0.5 Gaussian smoothing and missed the 5% matching
   tolerance. Diffusion uses 100,000 updates, not 10,000. These details now
   appear explicitly, with all 13 conditions, coefficient maps, objective
   reductions, attention detachment, donor construction and update budgets.
3. All twenty secondary RetinaNet outcomes are included. Counts from existing
   completed payload annotations reconcile 122,707 candidates with 122,702
   retained and five invalid detections discarded on five images. One completed
   cell was untouched; nineteen carry adapter annotations. Both W17 failure
   files remain preserved (two events with identical content hashes), as do
   all six cross-stage failure records. No inference or selection was repeated.
4. Primary bootstrap resampling of specimens and pipelines is distinguished
   from the low-data nine-cell bootstrap, which conditions on the fixed test
   cohort and ignores overlapping-subset dependence. W16 ranges are ranges,
   not CIs. The secondary architecture is descriptive, not another confirmatory
   test family. No locked statistical outputs were replaced.
5. Historical test-role reuse, the bounded identifier-display incident, lack of
   manufacturing-build identity, unverified inherited boxes, inaccessible
   source volumes and coarse-hash similarity flags are disclosed. The internal
   protocol freeze is not called prospective public preregistration.
6. Abstract, Results, Discussion and cover letter state both favourable and
   adverse directions. Figure 2 preserves all arms and adds explicitly
   different-scale non-ND detail, avoiding compression by the ND outlier scale.
   No illustrative or synthetic AI-generated image is used as experimental
   evidence. No licensed source-image example is newly republished.
7. Automated headings and checksums no longer alone produce the minor-revision
   verdict: a separately documented source-bound content review is required.
   Numeric audit recomputes paired means and quantiles from saved draws; it
   does not claim to rerun the excluded raw-prediction bootstrap. All final PDF
   pages must be visually inspected, not merely compiled.
